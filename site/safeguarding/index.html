Safeguarding

Stump’d safeguarding commitments and how to raise a concern.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Our commitment

The welfare of children and young people is paramount. Stump’d is committed to creating respectful, inclusive environments and responding appropriately to concerns.

How we work

  • We agree roles, supervision, venue arrangements and emergency contacts with the commissioning school, club or responsible adult.
  • We maintain appropriate professional boundaries and do not use private direct messaging with children.
  • We listen to concerns, record them factually and pass them promptly to the appropriate safeguarding lead or statutory service.
  • We expect adults involved in delivery to follow relevant codes of conduct, safer-working practices and required vetting arrangements.

Raising a concern

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999. If a child may be at risk but there is no immediate danger, contact the safeguarding lead at their school or club and the appropriate local authority children’s service. You can also contact the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000.

To report a concern connected with Stump’d, email hello@stumpd.co.uk with the subject “Safeguarding concern”. Do not use the general enquiry form. We will handle information on a need-to-know basis, but cannot promise confidentiality where sharing is necessary to protect a child.

Working with organisations

Before delivery, schools and clubs should identify their designated safeguarding lead, share relevant procedures and ensure appropriate supervision. Stump’d provision complements rather than replaces the organisation’s safeguarding responsibilities.
