Stump’d for schools

Stronger minds belong in every team.

Practical cricket-based programmes that help pupils understand pressure, build confidence and develop habits they can take back to the classroom.

Why it matters

More than a sports session.

Cricket gives pupils a safe, active way to experience challenge: waiting, failing, recovering, communicating and trying again.

We use those moments to develop self-awareness, emotional regulation and positive team behaviours. Sessions are inclusive and shaped around the age, experience and needs of your group.

What we deliver

A programme that fits your setting.

Discovery

We listen to your priorities, year groups and timetable before proposing the right format.

Delivery

Active, age-appropriate sessions combine cricket challenges with reflection and practical tools.

Carry-over

Shared language and simple prompts help staff and pupils keep using the learning.

Flexible formats

Built around the school day.

Primary schools

Accessible sessions for Years 5–6, introducing thoughts, feelings, confidence and the “next ball” response.

Secondary schools

Deeper work on focus, pressure, setbacks, communication and leadership for pupils aged 11–17.

Groups and teams

Options can support curriculum enrichment, wellbeing priorities, cricket teams, transition or targeted cohorts. We agree scope, group size and outcomes before delivery.
