Meet the team

People first. Players second.

Stump’d was created by Rob Osman and Jack Henderson to give young people practical support for the moments cricket—and life—ask the most of them.

Our story

Built from the game, for the person.

We know cricket can bring joy, belonging and confidence. We also know how exposed, uncertain and personal it can feel.

Stump’d brings coaching and conversation together. Our role is not to remove challenge or promise perfect confidence. It is to help young people understand themselves, make useful choices and keep moving forward.

RO

Co-founder

Rob Osman

Rob brings cricket experience and a belief that development begins with being curious about the person. His sessions make space for young people to talk, test ideas and find approaches that feel genuinely theirs.

JH

Co-founder

Jack Henderson

Jack combines an understanding of the game with practical, energetic coaching. He helps players translate broad ideas such as confidence and focus into actions they can use at training, in matches and beyond cricket.

How we work

Warm. Practical. Honest.

We listen first

The young person and their context come before a predetermined answer.

We make it usable

Every idea needs to survive contact with a real match, classroom or difficult day.

We keep perspective

Performance matters, but wellbeing, enjoyment and the person behind the player matter more.

We protect young people

Our work is guided by clear professional boundaries and safeguarding practice. Read our safeguarding information.
