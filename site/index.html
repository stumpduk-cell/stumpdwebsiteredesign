01 / The approach

Cricket asks difficult questions.

Mistakes happen in public. Confidence can disappear between deliveries. Emotions run high and pressure arrives without warning.

We help young people notice what is happening, respond with purpose and move forward—on the pitch and beyond it.

A simple tool for difficult moments

Step back.Take a breath.Next Ball.

You cannot change the last delivery. You can choose how you meet the next one.
