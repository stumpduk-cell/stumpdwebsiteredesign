Privacy

How Stump’d handles personal information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

About this notice

This notice explains how Stump’d handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us. Stump’d is responsible for deciding how that information is used.

Information we receive

We may receive your name, email address, organisation and anything you choose to include in an enquiry. This website does not store enquiry-form entries: the form prepares a message in your own email application. Our hosting provider may process limited technical data, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect the site.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond, discuss services, prepare proposals, administer agreed work and keep appropriate business records. We rely on taking steps at your request, performing a contract, and our legitimate interests in operating and safeguarding the service, as applicable.

Sharing and retention

We share information only with service providers needed to operate our communications and website, where required by law, or to protect a person from harm. We keep it only as long as necessary for the purpose, legal obligations and the resolution of disputes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to use of your information. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children’s information

Parents, carers, schools and clubs should avoid sending sensitive information about a child in an initial enquiry. We will explain any further information needed and how it will be handled.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@stumpd.co.uk.
