The Stump’d pathway

Grow the skill. Deepen the thinking.

A progressive route for ages 9–17, designed to make mental skills useful now and more independent over time.

Our method

Learn it. Try it. Own it.

Each stage meets young people at the right level, then returns to core ideas with greater challenge and responsibility.

Cricket is the practice ground: a place to notice thoughts and feelings, try a response and reflect without judgement.

Stage 01
Years 5–6 / 9–11

Spark

Make the invisible visible.

Young players discover how thoughts and feelings can affect actions. Playful challenges introduce simple reset routines, helpful self-talk and the idea of choosing a response.

  • Recognise thoughts, feelings and body signals
  • Respond to mistakes with “next ball” thinking
  • Speak and listen as part of a team

Tools to take away

Simple visual prompts help players notice a fast reaction, find some space and choose what to do next.

Stage 02
Ages 11–13

Foundation

Turn ideas into repeatable habits.

Players practise routines for preparation, concentration and recovery, and learn how communication can help both themselves and others.

  • Build a personal reset routine
  • Direct attention to what matters now
  • Understand controllables and helpful choices
Stage 03
Ages 14–17

Core

Apply skills independently under pressure.

Older players explore identity, expectations and leadership, selecting and adapting tools for the demands they face.

  • Prepare purposefully for different situations
  • Manage pressure and setbacks with perspective
  • Lead self and contribute to team culture

The Stump’d Five

A language that sticks.

Think

Notice the story you are telling yourself.

Reset

Create space between the moment and your response.

Focus

Put attention on the next useful action.

Communicate

Use clear words and listen with intent.

Lead

Take responsibility for your choices and your effect on others.
