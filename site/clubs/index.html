Stump’d for clubs

A shared language for the whole club.

Help junior players manage pressure, enjoy the game and grow—while giving coaches and parents practical ways to support them.

The opportunity

Coach the person, not only the shot.

Technical practice matters. But young players also need to recover from mistakes, stay present and communicate when the game gets difficult.

Stump’d brings those skills into the cricket environment. We work alongside your existing coaching, using language that is memorable, positive and easy to reinforce.

A whole-club approach

Learning that travels.

Players

Interactive sessions turn mental skills into routines players can test in practice and matches.

Coaches

Common prompts make it easier to create reflection, ownership and calm under pressure.

Parents

Clear principles help adults support development, enjoyment and perspective from the boundary.
