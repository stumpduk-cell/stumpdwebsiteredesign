The moments that matter

The game moves fast.
The mind can learn to reset.

Mistake. Pressure. Frustration. Embarrassment. A decision that did not go your way.

Stump’d gives young people simple tools to recognise what is happening, respond deliberately and focus on what comes next.

A simple idea that travels

Next Ball.

You cannot change what just happened. You can influence what you do next.

Not ignoring the emotion. Not pretending the mistake does not matter. Recognise it, reset and deliberately move your attention forwards.
