Why it matters

Develop better players by developing better thinkers.

Pressure does not only arrive in a match.

It shows up after a mistake, in the classroom, within a team and when confidence drops.

Stump’d gives young people simple ways to recognise what is happening, reset and choose what they do next.

Cricket has always trained hands.
Stump’d trains minds.

  1. Think.
  2. Reset.
  3. Focus.
  4. Communicate.
  5. Lead.
